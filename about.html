A NOTE FROM THE ROOM

Sharp tools.
Soft ego.

NIGHTSHIFT is a small online learning room for people who want to study ethical hacking with patience, context, and a healthy respect for other people's systems.

The lessons are designed around a simple idea: technical confidence grows faster when the environment is safe enough to ask “why?” We use deliberately vulnerable examples, clear boundaries, and practical exercises that leave you with something you can explain.
